      Daily goals

      Estimates from standard formulas (Mifflin-St Jeor for calories, body-weight ranges for protein and water). They're a starting point, not medical advice. Check with a doctor if you're pregnant, under 18, or managing a health condition.

      Food search

      Sources, learning and saved results

      Searches check what you've taught it, your saved foods, about 3,200 built-in everyday foods (USDA), common restaurant items, the USDA FoodData Central database (brand-name and generic foods), and Open Food Facts. USDA's shared demo key allows about 30 searches an hour. For more, get a free key at api.data.gov/signup and paste it here.
