      Food search

      Where lookups come from

      Searches check your saved foods, a built-in list of common restaurant items, the USDA FoodData Central database (brand-name and generic foods), and Open Food Facts. USDA's shared demo key allows about 30 searches an hour. For more, get a free key at api.data.gov/signup and paste it here.

      Your data lives in this browser

      Clearing browser data erases your log. Export a backup now and then, and import it on a new phone or browser.
